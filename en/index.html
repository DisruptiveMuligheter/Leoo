    Postcards from the Future

    We live in an exponential world, and those with good mental maps see the opportunities first. In the book, Audun Wickstrand Iversen and Leo Rundgren Olsen gather what you need to draw those maps, told as a story. It follows four technologies that will change everyday life and the economy, humanoid robots, AI agents, voice technology and self-driving vehicles.

    Along the way come 14 postcards from 2032, written by future versions of well-known Norwegians. The book is published in Norwegian as Postkort fra fremtiden.
